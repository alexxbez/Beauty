Our story

A house built on stillness.

Maison Éclat began in 1998 with a single treatment room, a pot of rose cream, and a belief that beauty should feel like relief.

Our founder, Élise Marchand, trained as a formulator before she trained as an aesthetician. That order still shapes everything we do: we begin with the skin, the scent, and the nervous system — not with a trend.

Today the atelier is still small by choice. We keep our appointment book generous, our formulations deliberate, and our door open to anyone who wants a quiet hour.

What we keep

Three quiet commitments.

Considered formulations

Plant-led, small-batch, and transparent. We choose fewer ingredients and give each one a reason to be there.

Unhurried service

No double bookings, no rushed consultations. Your appointment includes the time it actually takes.

Radical welcome

Every body, every skin, every budget. Accessibility is not an add-on; it is part of our hospitality.

A short history

Measured in seasons.

  1. 1998

    The first room

    Élise opens a single treatment room in the Marais with a waiting list of twelve.

  2. 2006

    The atelier

    The house moves to Rue des Fleurs and adds a small formulation kitchen.

  3. 2014

    The collection

    Éclat No. 1 Serum is released after four years of quiet testing.

  4. 2022

    A wider welcome

    We begin an accessibility review of every part of the atelier, from the doorway to the website.

  5. 2026

    Still small

    The appointment book is open, the kettle is on, and the door is yours.
