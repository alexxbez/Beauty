Our formulation promise

Fewer things, chosen well.

Plant-led

Cold-pressed oils, botanical waxes, and extracts chosen for how they feel on skin, not for a label.

Small-batch

Made in short runs so nothing sits in a warehouse. Every batch is dated and traceable.

Refillable

Glass where we can, refills where it makes sense, and packaging that is easy to recycle.

Cruelty-free

Never tested on animals, and never sold in markets that require it.

Fragrance transparency

We list every scent note and avoid hiding behind the word “parfum”.

Dermatologist-tested

Tested on a range of skin types, including sensitive and reactive skin.
