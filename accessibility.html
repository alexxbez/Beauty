Accessibility

A welcome that includes you.

We want Maison Éclat to be a place — and a website — that more people can use comfortably. This page explains the tools available and how to reach us if something is not working.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. That is a target, not a claim of perfection. We review the site regularly, test it with keyboard navigation and screen readers, and fix what we find.

Accessibility is part of our hospitality, not a separate project. The same care that goes into a quiet treatment room goes into clear headings, visible focus, readable text, and honest alternatives.

Tools on this site

Adjust the page to suit you.

Open the Accessibility button at the bottom-right of any page. Your choices are saved on this device and follow you between pages.

  • Easy-read font

    Replaces the decorative serif with a clear sans-serif, removes decorative italics, and opens up the line spacing.

  • Text size

    Five steps from 100% to 140%. The current size is announced as you change it.

  • High contrast

    Black text on white surfaces, stronger borders, underlined links, and a highly visible focus ring.

  • Reduce motion

    Stops transitions and smooth scrolling. The site also respects your system’s reduced-motion setting on first visit.

  • Read aloud

    Reads the main content using your browser’s speech voice. Select a passage first to hear only that passage.

  • Voice dictation

    On the contact page, a microphone button adds spoken words to the message field. Availability depends on your browser.

Keyboard and screen reader

A clear path through the page.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves through links, buttons, and form fields.
  • Shift + Tab moves backwards.
  • Enter or Space activates a button.
  • Escape closes the accessibility panel.
  • The first link on every page is Skip to main content.

Screen readers

The site uses one main landmark, one primary navigation landmark, logical headings, descriptive link text, and labelled form fields. Decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology; meaningful images have text alternatives.

If a label, heading, or announcement does not make sense with your screen reader, please tell us. That is useful information, not a complaint.

Browser support

What works where.

Support for the accessibility tools in current browsers. “Varies” means the feature may depend on the operating system or browser settings.
Tool Support Notes
Easy-read font, text size, high contrast, reduced motion All modern browsers Built with HTML, CSS, and a small amount of JavaScript for saving preferences.
Read aloud Most modern browsers Uses the browser’s speech synthesis. Voice availability and quality vary by device.
Voice dictation Varies Best supported in Chromium-based browsers. If unavailable, the message field always works by typing.
Form validation All modern browsers Uses native HTML validation with visible labels and hints.

Known limitations

Where we are still working.

  • The contact form is a demonstration. It validates your details and shows a confirmation, but it does not send a message. Use the direct email link to reach us.
  • Voice dictation and read aloud depend on browser and operating-system support. They are enhancements, not requirements for using the site.
  • Speech recognition may send audio to your browser vendor for processing. If that is not comfortable, typing is always available.
  • The photographs carry descriptive alternative text. Decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology, and the stylised map has a text description.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Feedback

Tell us what would help.

If you meet a barrier on this site, or if something could be clearer, please write to us. We aim to reply within five working days and will tell you what we can change.
