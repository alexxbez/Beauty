What to expect

A rhythm, not a routine.

1. The conversation

We begin with tea and a few questions about your skin, your senses, and the week you have had.

2. The ritual

Your practitioner works at a measured pace, explaining each step only if you want to know it.

3. The aftercare

You leave with a short, realistic plan — never a shelf of products you will not use.
